# BeirutPoly

**BeirutPoly** is a fictional monopoly simulation built around **Beirut, Lebanon**, where the player controls a dominant business serving the city's market.

The game uses familiar **Beirut locations, neighborhoods, businesses, and consumer behavior** to turn the classic economics of a monopoly into an engaging local scenario.

As the sole major supplier in its market, BeirutPoly faces **downward-sloping demand**, allowing the player to influence the price and quantity it sells. The player must balance:

- 💰 Pricing
- 📦 Production and operating costs
- 📈 Customer demand
- 🏢 Market power
- 💵 Profit maximization
- 👥 Consumer welfare

BeirutPoly can be used to demonstrate key economics concepts such as **marginal revenue, marginal cost, profit maximization, consumer surplus, producer surplus, and deadweight loss**, while using Beirut as a familiar and relatable setting.

## Tagline

> **"Own the market. Set the price. Run Beirut."**
